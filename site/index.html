        How we work

        Seven steps from first chat to keys.

        One project lead stays with you throughout, and you get photos from site every week.

        Timings are typical for a 3 BHK and above full-home interior.

        1. Consultation

          Day 1

          A relaxed meeting at your site, our studio or on video. We talk about how you live, your budget and your timeline.

        2. Site measurement

          Week 1

          We measure every room, check walls, plumbing and electrical points, and photograph the space.

        3. Layout and moodboard

          Weeks 1–2

          Furniture layouts and a design board with colours, finishes and materials for each room.

        4. 3D views

          Weeks 2–4

          Photoreal 3D views of each key room. We refine them with you until everything feels right.

        5. Drawings and quotation

          Weeks 4–5

          Working drawings for every carpenter and electrician, plus a line-by-line quotation you approve.

        6. Execution

          Weeks 5–14

          Our team builds on site while your project lead shares progress photos every week.

        7. Styling and handover

          Final week

          Snags fixed, décor and artwork in place, then a full walkthrough of your new home.

        Questions

        Before you book.

        Anything else, ask us in the consultation. It is free, with no obligation to go ahead.

        Do you design buildings, or only interiors?

        Both. We design new bungalows, villas, row houses and farmhouses from the plot up, and we design and build interiors for apartments and homes across Pune. Many clients take both, so one team carries the story from plan to painting.

        Can you paint a mural or artwork for my home?

        Yes. Our founder, Ar. Jayesh Lunawat, creates custom paintings, murals and artworks for the homes we design. We also style homes with décor and soft furnishings, and design furniture to order.

        How much does a full-home interior cost?

        It depends on the size of your home, the rooms you want done and the materials you choose. After the site visit we share a line-by-line quotation, so you can see exactly where the budget goes before anything starts.

        How long will my home take?

        A 2 or 3 BHK full-home interior usually takes 8 to 12 weeks on site once drawings are approved. Larger homes, renovations and new buildings take longer; you get a written schedule before work begins.

        Do you take projects outside Pune?

        Yes. Our studio is in Pune and we take on projects across India. Our team travels for site visits, design reviews can happen on video, and you get the same weekly photo updates wherever your home is.
